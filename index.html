Event checklist

What I need from you so your event runs smoothly.

Power and space

A safe, stable table and a working power socket close to where I set up, with enough room for my equipment.

Timing

Tell me the start time, finish time and any key moments (entrance, speeches, cake, first dance) so the music matches the programme.

Setup and sound check

I need access to the venue early to set up and test the sound before guests arrive.

Song requests and do-not-play list

Send me your must-play songs and anything you do not want played. I will mix your favourites into the set.

Venue details

Share the exact location and a contact person at the venue, so I can arrive without delays.
